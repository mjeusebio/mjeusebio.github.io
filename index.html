Questions

Before you
send a brief

Which platforms do you cut for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts, all vertical 9:16. Other ratios on request.

What do you need from me?

Your product, who it is for and what you want people to do after watching. A script is welcome but not needed.

Do I get more than one version?

Yes. Each ad ships with several hooks, and recipe reels can be made with several presenters, so you can test what wins.

Can you edit footage I already have?

Yes. I review the raw clips, plan the hook, body and closer, then cut, caption and export the ad.

Which tools do you use?

Claude, Google Flow, Higgsfield, Adobe Firefly, Premiere Pro, Photoshop and Lightroom, picking the model that suits each shot.
